General Settings

Basic information shown across the UniGuard system.

Displayed in the sidebar and page titles.

Security Settings

Rules that control how accounts authenticate and stay signed in.

Session Timeout
Automatically log out inactive users after this period.
Minimum Password Length
Required for all new and updated account passwords.
Require Two-Factor Authentication
Enforce 2FA for Admin and Chief Security Officer accounts.
Lock Account After Failed Attempts
Temporarily lock an account after repeated failed logins.

Notification Settings

Email Alerts for Critical Incidents
Notify the CSO and Admin team immediately.
SMS Alerts for Failed Logins
Send an SMS when repeated failed login attempts are detected.
Daily Activity Summary Email
A digest of the day's system activity, sent to admins.
