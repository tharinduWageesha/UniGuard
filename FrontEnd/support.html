Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a critical incident report?
Navigate to the Incident Management page in your portal, click "Report New Incident", fill in the sector location, priority level, and detailed summary, then click Submit.
What should I do if a vehicle QR code scanner fails at the gate?
Duty JSOs can switch to manual barcode input or lookup the vehicle registration number directly in the Vehicle Records directory on their dashboard.
How long does it take for Visitor Approvals to process?
Visitor pass requests are reviewed in real-time by the OIC and University Management. Urgent passes are processed within 15–30 minutes.
How can I update my profile picture or account details?
Go to "My Profile" from the top-right user menu. Click "Upload New Photo" or edit your contact information, then click "Save Profile Changes".
Who do I contact if my account is deactivated?
Account status changes are managed by the System Administrator. Submit a support ticket below or contact admin@uniguard.lk.
